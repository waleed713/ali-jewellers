Measuring a ring

Take a ring that already fits the finger. Measure the inside width in millimetres and match it to the ring chart. No ring? Wrap a thin strip of paper around the finger, mark where it meets and measure the length.

Measuring a bangle

Bring your thumb and little finger together and measure across the widest part of your hand, at the knuckles. Pick the bangle whose inside diameter is just above that number.

Measuring a chain

Lay a piece of string around your neck at the length you like, then measure the string against a ruler. Chains are measured end to end, clasp included.

Questions people ask

How accurate is the estimated weight?

The estimate is within about 5%. The exact weight depends on the final design, and we confirm it with you before we start making the piece.

How many grams is a tola?

One tola is 11.664 grams. The chart shows both grams and tola.

Which karat should I choose?

22K is the usual choice for jewellery in Pakistan. 18K is harder and holds stones better. 24K is pure gold but soft, so it suits simple plain pieces.

Can I order a size that is not on the chart?

Yes. Message us with the size or send a photo of a piece that fits you and we will make it to match.

How long does a custom piece take?

Most custom pieces take 2 to 4 weeks. Bridal sets can take up to 8 weeks.
