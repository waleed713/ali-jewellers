Which gold should you pick?

24K, for saving

Pure gold, but soft. Best kept as bars, coins or simple plain bands.

22K, for jewellery

What most bridal and festive sets are made of. Rich colour and strong enough to wear often.

18K, for daily wear

A harder alloy that holds stones well and shrugs off scratches.
